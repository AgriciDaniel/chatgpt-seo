# SEO-pro privacy statement

Publisher: Daniel Agrici. Effective date: 19 October 2026.

This statement covers the skills-only SEO-pro package. It includes instructions,
references, templates, and offline file-processing scripts. It has no
publisher-operated server, account system, analytics, tracking pixel, lead form,
or network-capable helper. Daniel Agrici receives no task inputs or results
through this package and retains no publisher copy of that task data.

## Inputs and purposes

You select the page, draft, screenshot, image, markup, or export needed for an
SEO task. Inputs may include business information, website URLs, search queries,
metrics, author names, or public contact details. They are used to inspect the
selected evidence and prepare requested findings, drafts, visuals, or plans.
No email address or publisher account is needed for supplied-file workflows.

Use only the material necessary for your task. Remove unrelated personal data.
Do not supply authentication secrets, payment-card data, government identifiers,
or private health records. Use the host's connection flow for any separately
authorized account access.

## Processing and recipients

ChatGPT and tools you authorize process selected inputs under their own terms,
privacy settings, and retention policies. Browsing may contact the public site
you ask to inspect. Requested image creation or editing uses the host's
integrated image tool when available. An independently connected account tool
can return data only within its authorized scope.

The offline helpers process selected files in the execution environment. They
do not fetch URLs or transmit files to the publisher or another recipient.
The package does not automatically send a brief, register a lead, or transmit
contact information when a publisher link is followed.

## Retention and controls

Publisher retention of task inputs through the package is none: there is no
publisher storage service or task-data collection endpoint. This does not mean
that your host retains nothing. Inputs and generated artifacts may remain in
your chat, execution environment, or workspace according to the host's settings
and your file choices. The package does not set or extend those periods.

You can use supplied files instead of connected accounts, decline a tool,
redact exports before uploading, manage chats and artifacts through the host's
controls, remove local outputs, and uninstall the plugin. Consult your host
or tool provider for its applicable retention and deletion controls.

## GitHub support

Support is handled through the project's GitHub issue tracker. Information you
choose to post there is separate from plugin task processing and may include
your GitHub identity, version, a redacted example, and troubleshooting details.
In the public repository, issues and comments are public. Daniel Agrici and
other readers can view them; GitHub hosts them under its own policies.

Support records can remain available in GitHub until edited or removed through
GitHub's supported controls. No fixed publisher deletion schedule, private
inbox, or automatic deletion service is offered by this package. Do not post
sensitive data. For a privacy question, open a high-level issue without the
underlying private material and request an appropriate reporting route.
