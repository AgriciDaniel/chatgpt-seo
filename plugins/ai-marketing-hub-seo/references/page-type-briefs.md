# Page-type content briefs

Choose section purposes from the reader's actual task. Outline length and
section choices follow the evidence needed; avoid fixed word counts, keyword
density, mandatory FAQ blocks, assumed proof and default lead-capture CTAs.
Use [the brief worksheet](../assets/content-brief.md).

| Page | Useful section decisions | Required facts and proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explanation / guide | Direct answer, scope, mechanisms, examples, limits and next learning step | Reliable definitions, examples and current authoritative facts |
| How-to | Prerequisites, ordered actions, actual examples, failure/recovery paths | A verified process, known permissions/tools and truthful outcomes |
| Comparison / alternatives | Buyer criteria, factual differences, fit by need and tradeoffs | Current inspected product facts, dates and fair comparison scope |
| Product / service | Actual offer, who it suits, decision factors, delivery/limitations and next step | Known features, genuine price/policies, supported proof and capacity |
| Location | Real availability, local customer needs, service process and contact details | Actual service area/premises and useful local specificity |
| Category / buying guide | Choice architecture, differentiators, fit, filtering and useful products | Genuine inventory, category criteria and appropriate product facts |
| Case study | Context, intervention, measured result, method, limitations and permission | Real attributable evidence; no fabricated client results |
| Landing / pricing / demo | Promise-to-page fit, offer clarity, objections and appropriate action | Actual campaign/offer terms and known measurement events |
| Topic hub | Clear theme, useful subtopics, navigation and ongoing maintenance | Actual page inventory, intent relationships and update responsibility |

Each brief should include audience/locale, task/intent, target queries with
measurement provenance or hypothesis labels, known offer facts, section
purposes, claim/source needs, title/H1/description options, internal-link
targets, visual requirements, candidate markup and acceptance checks.

Research current search examples when tools permit. Do not invent keyword
volume, difficulty or a measured competitor gap from a bare topic. When evidence
is limited, deliver a provisional brief and a specific research plan instead
of blocking useful structural work.

New imagery can use [native image generation](native-images.md). For each
section, keep the original practical information needed by the reader and use
placeholders for facts the user has not provided.
