# Link evidence and topic architecture

## Backlink analysis

Record the supplied profile's provider/method, dates, coverage, URL/domain
grain and fields. Separate discovered links, actually fetched linking pages,
observed anchors, relation attributes, destinations/status and missing data.
An authority metric is the source's proprietary diagnostic, not Google's score.
No backlink provider or entitlement is bundled.

Review relevance, actual context, natural link placement, source legitimacy,
destination intent, repeated patterns and evidence of paid/spam behavior. Avoid
automatic toxicity labels, disavow recommendations or claims of lost traffic
from an export alone. Link removal, outreach, paid placements and disavow
submission require their specific scope. A public contact address is not
authorization to contact someone.

For competitor link opportunities, inspect comparable pages and explain why a
resource could serve that audience. Use genuine existing content and proof.
Do not invent acquired links, email replies, conversions or an outreach run.

## Topic clustering

Use actual query/intent evidence, locale, existing URLs and business relevance.
With bare keyword lists, produce provisional semantic/intent groups and mark
demand and SERP overlap unmeasured. Do not pretend a similarity algorithm
measures ranking competition. With inspected SERPs, record query pairs, engine,
date, sample size, normalization and common results before applying an overlap
rule. Thresholds are configurable analytical choices, not ranking laws.

Decide whether a group needs one page, useful variants or separate pages based
on reader tasks and observed evidence. Select hub/spoke relationships and
internal-link placements from actual page purpose. Existing multiple URLs are
not automatically cannibalization; confirm comparable query/page performance,
canonical intent and content fit before proposing consolidation.

Produce a page map with intent, query group, existing/proposed URL, supporting
facts, internal links, hypothesis/evidence level and next check. Use
[clustering](clustering.md) for the method and [strategy archetypes](strategy-archetypes.md)
for business-model choices. Compare equivalent cohorts after implementation;
content publication does not establish indexing or ranking.

## Normalization and ambiguous cases

Retain source page, target URL, referring domain, anchor/image alt, relation
attributes, placement, first/last observed dates, status and source method.
Deduplicate page-level observations while retaining template repetition as a
concentration signal. Group anchors without healthy-percentage quotas. Verify
source pages as present, absent, removed, blocked or unverified; a source's lost
label belongs to its observation window. Compatible dated snapshots are needed
for trends.

Record informational, commercial-investigation, transactional or navigational
intent, allowing mixed intent. Preserve dominant result types/features and the
actual shared URLs. For ambiguous overlap, inspect task/format differences and
same-domain/different-page patterns; run a sensitivity check when ubiquitous
domains dominate. Record adjacent pages' distinct task, audience, stage, format
or entity. Links should serve reader transitions, without anchor/count quotas.
