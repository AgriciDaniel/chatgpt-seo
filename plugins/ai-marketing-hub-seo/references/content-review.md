# Content evidence and editorial review

Load for content/page review, rewriting and substantial briefs. Content quality
needs editorial judgment and inspected facts; a word-count script cannot prove
experience, expertise, originality or audience usefulness.

## Who, how and why

Identify the author/organization responsible for consequential claims, the
process used to produce the content and the reader's intended task. Seek
actual first-hand examples, methods, sources, product experience and proof.
Do not invent author qualifications, test results or personal experience.
AI assistance alone does not establish usefulness or a quality defect.
Detector scores do not prove authorship or search-policy compliance.

| Review area | Concrete questions | Useful output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intent | What decision or task does the audience need to complete? Which locale and stage? | Purpose statement and known/hypothesized queries |
| Specificity | What information could only come from the product, practitioner or actual evidence? | Missing fact requests and usable supplied detail |
| Claim support | Which claims are supported, stale, promotional, disputed or unverified? | Claim ledger with source, date, locator and confidence |
| Structure | Do headings, answers, examples and next steps help the reader navigate? | Purpose-based outline and clear section transitions |
| Metadata | Do title and description represent the actual page and useful differentiator? | Truthful alternatives; character previews are display heuristics |
| Trust | Are authors, contact/support context, corrections and relevant evidence available? | Evidence gaps without invented credentials or required badges |
| Multimedia | Does each visual clarify the material? Is its purpose conveyed accessibly? | Actual asset review or requested native-image workflow |
| Internal links | Which real pages help the next task or explain prerequisites? | Suggested placements and verified destinations |
| Freshness | Which facts, product details and dates require an update? | Specific refresh tasks; do not change dates without substantive work |
| Conversion fit | Is the next action appropriate to the reader's stage and the genuine offer? | CTA/objection hypotheses, verified proof and measurement gaps |

For YMYL material, factual review and suitable accountable expertise are central.
Use current authoritative sources for medical, financial or legal claims. Do
not manufacture qualifications or present a content score as professional
review. Inspect sourced page claims in context and distinguish obligation from
general publishing practice.

## Rewriting and content families

Preserve the user's offer, tone, original facts and intended audience. Keep
useful source material; remove unsupported duplication and add missing decision
information only from evidence. Mark missing facts rather than filling them.
Measure performance on comparable scope only. Low traffic does not by itself
prove bad content, and high volume does not justify a new page without intent.

For scaled pages, evaluate dataset reliability, actual utility, material
differences, discoverability and update ownership. Pilot representative page
types before expansion. Location/token swaps and arbitrary uniqueness
percentages cannot establish quality. Consider consolidating pages when the
same task is genuinely served, preserving important existing contracts.

Use [page-type briefs](page-type-briefs.md) for section decisions and
[backlinks and clustering](backlinks-clusters.md) for architecture. Optional
editorial scores must identify the assessed rubric and unknown dimensions.
Use the package health score only under its separate evidence gates.
