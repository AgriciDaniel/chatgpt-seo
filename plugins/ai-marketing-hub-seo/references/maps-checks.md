# Local business and Maps evidence

Use public/supplied business facts, profile screenshots, authorized account
read results and suitable rank-grid exports. The package includes no live
grid provider. Do not infer business details or measurements from competitors.

| Area | Inspect | Decision and evidence limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | Real-world name, address/service-area status, phone, website and profile URLs | Reconcile documented inconsistencies; do not keyword-stuff a business name |
| Categories | Actual services and available profile category choices | Recommend a truthful fit after checking current choices and policy |
| Services / description | Verified offering, geography, genuine differentiators and restrictions | Draft useful accurate wording; no unsupported awards, prices or guarantees |
| Hours | Normal/special hours and appointment requirements | Keep factual hours; never invent extended availability to pursue a ranking claim |
| Reviews | Supplied dates, ratings, texts, responses and source completeness | Separate observed themes/velocity from hypotheses; do not label reviews fake from text alone |
| Images | Actual premises/products or clearly identified generated concepts | Use native generation for requested concepts; do not pass synthetic scenes off as real evidence |
| Website | Service/location content, contact paths, accessibility and conversion tasks | Connect changes to inspected user needs and known business facts |
| Local markup | Genuine identity and visible page agreement | Review appropriate LocalBusiness vocabulary and current eligibility separately |
| Competitors | Inspected comparable profiles/pages in the stated geography | Date observations; do not infer revenue, authority or account metrics |

## Geo-grid reporting

Record query, language, location, grid center/spacing, timestamps, devices,
rank cutoff and provider/export method if supplied. Distinguish sampled cells,
observed placements, cells beyond a reporting cutoff, errors and missing rows.
Calculate coverage and averages only with explicit denominators; do not turn
missing data into a rank zero. Keep query/device/time cohorts separate.
Weighted figures need a documented weighting choice. Use
[the local-grid worksheet](../assets/local-grid-review.md).

Compare equivalent grids before calling a movement. Distance and sampling
differences can explain observation changes. Correlation does not establish
that a profile edit caused ranking or leads. Reviews, posting cadence and
profile fields should be assessed for genuine usefulness and policy, without
unsupported direct-ranking promises.

Profile edits, review responses, solicitation and publication require the
user's relevant action scope. A draft and a supplied-data analysis can proceed
without contacting anyone. Primary source for live platform rules:
[Google business representation guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177).

## Business shape and branches

First distinguish a customer-facing physical location, a service-area business
that travels to customers, and a hybrid. Apply physical address/map checks only
where that model calls for them; a legitimately hidden service-area address is
not a defect. Derive a LocalBusiness subtype from actual services. For branches,
verify individual location URLs, genuine local facts, navigation and unique
entity identifiers. A location-name swap needs useful local differentiation,
not an arbitrary uniqueness percentage.
