# Search experience and decision fit

Use inspected page/search evidence and known customer goals. A classification
is a working hypothesis, not a universal ranking label. A page can serve more
than one useful task. Validate the dominant task against actual audience and
search examples rather than forcing it into one template.

## Review dimensions

- Reader stage: exploring a problem, understanding choices, comparing an offer,
  selecting a provider, completing a transaction or using a product.
- Page job: topic explanation, how-to, comparison, product/category, service,
  location, pricing, resource, landing page or navigation hub.
- User story: "As [known audience], I need [task], so I can [desired outcome]."
  Use supplied audience research; mark an inferred persona as provisional.
- Decision information: known questions, constraints, objections, evidence,
  relevant examples, effort/risk and an appropriate next action.
- Usability: readable hierarchy, findable answers, accessible controls, mobile
  behavior, real navigation and task completion in the tested environment.
- Measurement: observed search-to-page expectations, actual engagement and
  qualified conversion events with dates/denominators, or a clear data gap.

Persona fit can be reviewed by need, familiarity, urgency, risk and decision
criteria. Scores are optional review aids; do not weight an invented persona
by guessed search volume or declare that a mismatch prevents ranking.

## Current and proposed page outline

Record the current sections and what each contributes to the task. Identify
missing decision information, distraction and unsupported proof. Propose a
sequence that resolves the actual reader questions with known evidence. Use
placeholders for missing facts, not invented logos, star ratings, customer
counts, certifications, scarcity, prices or testimonials.

Connect every recommendation to an evidence ID or explicit hypothesis. For a
task-flow change, describe its trigger, current behavior, desired behavior and
retest. A wireframe or copy proposal does not prove conversion uplift.

## Source taxonomy and persona cards

The eight source types are Landing Page, Blog Post, Product Page, Hybrid
(Service + Content), Service Page, Comparison Page, Local Page, and Tool /
Interactive. Record secondary signals and confidence; do not default an
uncertain page to a blog or require universal sections/markup from a label.

Preserve the four review dimensions: relevance to the task, clarity of needed
information, trustworthy supporting evidence and fit of the next action. A
persona card records role/situation, goal, journey stage, key questions and
derivation evidence. Emotional state is an evidence-backed hypothesis.
