# SEO strategy by business model

Select an archetype from known business facts, then adapt it to the actual
offer and audience. These page families and KPIs are choices to assess, not
mandatory site structures or quotas. Use [the strategy worksheet](../assets/strategy-plan.md).

| Archetype | Page families to assess | Evidence/proof needed | Measurement candidates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SaaS | Product/use cases, documentation, integrations, comparisons, pricing, support content | Actual features, audience needs, working integrations, genuine pricing, product experience | Qualified demo/signup, activation and retained users where data exists |
| Ecommerce | Useful categories, product/variant pages, buying guides, shipping/returns, comparisons | Real inventory, offers, variant distinctions, fulfillment policies, genuine reviews | Product discovery, purchase progression, qualified revenue and returns |
| Local service | Services, real service locations, contact/booking, practitioner evidence, profile-linked pages | Actual geography, capacity, public business facts, legitimate service proof | Qualified calls/bookings, lead quality and fulfilled jobs |
| Publisher | Topic hubs, articles, author/editorial information, resources and appropriate archives | Editorial purpose, original reporting, accountable sources, update process | Relevant readership, return visits and genuine subscriptions/conversion |
| Agency | Real capabilities, industries, case studies, people, process and contact paths | Genuine client permission, measured results, team qualifications and deliverables | Qualified enquiries, sales progression and suitable engagements |
| General business | Offer/task pages, useful resources, organization/contact details | Known audience, actual offer, differentiation and reliable evidence | Task completion and business outcomes suited to the stated model |

## Planning sequence

Define a business outcome, known baseline and constraints. Review existing
pages/contracts, accessible evidence, competitors and actual search tasks.
Choose architecture and content opportunities with an intent/demand rationale.
Identify dependencies: reliable facts, account/export data, site controls,
rendering, measurement and implementation capacity. Prioritize blockers before
scaled production. Use estimated effort and dates as assumptions tied to the
team's real capacity.

Define a pilot and acceptance criteria for programmatic or multilingual page
families. Useful distinct information and user demand matter more than fixed
page counts, word counts or uniqueness percentages. Keep publication cadence
proportional to the ability to produce and maintain evidence-based content.

Specify how each task will be verified and who can carry it out. Keep SEO
diagnostics distinct from forecast traffic/revenue. A roadmap cannot guarantee
rankings or tell a team to build unsupported schema, fake proof or a purchase.
