# FLOW Win: search and AI discovery review

Adapted from Daniel Agrici's Dual-Surface Content Scorecard, CC BY 4.0:
https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/flow.

Inputs: actual page copy, target tasks/queries, audience, genuine first-party
insights, conversion goal, accessible comparison pages and measured outcomes
if available.

Review original usefulness, audience/stage fit, evidence and specificity,
direct buyer answers, appropriate next steps, refresh needs and measurement
readiness. Evaluate traditional search, AI answer readiness and conversion
fit separately. Citation/ranking/conversion outcomes need actual observations.

Return evidence strengths, gaps, risks and the next three useful actions.
Optional rubric scores are editorial diagnostics with unknown dimensions
explicit. Do not convert unmeasured visibility into a score or override the
package's full SEO-health-score coverage gate.
