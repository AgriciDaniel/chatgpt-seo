# FLOW Win: conversion path review

Adapted from Daniel Agrici's Conversion Audit Prompt, CC BY 4.0:
https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/flow.

Inputs: actual page/funnel copy, source promise, conversion goal, comparable
analytics and first-party conversations/feedback where supplied.

Review expectation-to-offer fit, unanswered decision questions, objections,
customer language, CTA/task friction, tracking across form/phone/chat/offline
outcomes and whether reporting reaches qualified leads or sales. Keep measured
dropoff distinct from a copy/usability hypothesis. Do not recommend spending
merely because traffic exists.

Return observed blockers, lead-quality risks supported by data, messaging and
page fixes, measurement gaps, first-party evidence to collect and a ranked
experiment backlog with acceptance criteria. Draft only; do not contact leads.
