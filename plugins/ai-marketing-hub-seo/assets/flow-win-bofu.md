# FLOW Win: buyer decision brief

Adapted from Daniel Agrici's BOFU Page Brief Generator, CC BY 4.0:
https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/flow.

Offer / audience / journey stage: [known facts].
Immediate problem: [customer task and evidence].
Decision criteria: [what this audience needs to choose].
Objections: [supplied conversations/reviews or clearly marked hypotheses].
Customer language: [source-supported phrasing; no invented quotes].
Proof: [actual supporting material or gaps].

Deliver a page goal, intent, buyer questions, truthful H1, section purposes,
proof requirements, appropriate CTA, useful FAQ candidates and a measurement
plan. Connect tracking to genuine form/call/chat and qualified outcomes when
data is available. Do not invent proof or force a lead form for every page.
